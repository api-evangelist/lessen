# Lessen

> Lessen is a Scottsdale, Arizona property services and facilities management technology company connecting commercial and residential property owners and managers with a nationwide network of vetted service providers for repairs, maintenance, turns, make-readies and capital projects. Lessen publishes NO public API, developer portal, API reference or OpenAPI definition. Integrations with partner platforms are private partner connections, not a self-serve API program.
