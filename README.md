# Lessen

Lessen is a Scottsdale, Arizona based property services and facilities management technology company that connects commercial and residential property owners and managers with a nationwide network of vetted service providers for repairs, maintenance, turns, make-readies and capital projects. Its platform stack includes One by Lessen (work orders, vendor sourcing, invoicing), Lessen 360 (multifamily maintenance), Lessen Pro (affiliate contractors), Aiden (AI-assisted work-order creation) and the Lessen Marketplace (procurement). Lessen acquired SMS Assist in 2023 and is backed by Fifth Wall and General Catalyst.

**API surface: none public.** Lessen publishes no developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI definition, SDKs, CLI, sandbox, changelog, webhook catalog or MCP server. Its named platform integrations (Yardi, ServiceChannel, HappyCo, Property Meld, Avalara, Google Maps and others) are described as private partner connections with no published technical detail.
